Corrugated packaging, drawn from the dieline up.

I’m Saleh, a packaging designer from Lahore with three years of experience designing and producing corrugated boxes. I’m preparing to train as a Packmitteltechnologe in Germany.

See my projects Get in touch

About me

I’ve worked in my family’s corrugated box factory, Alwassey Packages, in R&D and IT support, and I design packaging that is ready to produce: correct dielines, sensible board choices and samples that fit the product.

What I’m aiming for

An Ausbildung as Packmitteltechnologe (packaging technologist) in Germany. I want to learn the full production chain, from board and printing to cutting dies and quality control, and build a career in the packaging industry.

Corrugated inlays for routers

  • ProductInternet routers
  • MaterialCorrugated board, die-cut
  • ReplacesCustom Styrofoam inlays

The factory had no way to make custom Styrofoam inlays, so I designed inlays for routers from die-cut corrugated sheets. They protect the product, cost less, and can be recycled with the box.
